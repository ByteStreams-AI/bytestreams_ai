Ask Your Data

Plain-English questions over your company database.

Letting AI write SQL against raw tables produces wrong numbers too often. We route every question through a semantic layer that defines your metrics, joins and business terms — so answers are accurate, and a question that can't be answered returns an error instead of a wrong number. Ideal for finance and operations teams with an existing database or warehouse.

MCP Connectors

Connect your business systems to AI assistants — safely.

The Model Context Protocol (MCP) is the open standard for connecting AI apps to databases and business software, replacing one-off integrations. We build MCP servers that never touch your live production database: read-only replicas, access limited to specific tables, and audit logging on every request.
