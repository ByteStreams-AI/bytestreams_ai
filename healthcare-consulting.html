Payer deadline: Jan 1, 2027

Electronic Prior Authorization Readiness

Get ready for the electronic prior-auth rules before they hit your workflow.

Payers' prior-authorization APIs are due January 1, 2027, and providers face the required MIPS electronic prior-auth measure in 2028. For practices and specialty pharmacies, we start with a fixed-fee assessment of your prior-auth volume, turnaround times, denial reasons and readiness for each submission channel, then implement the fixes. For mid-size Medicaid plans, we build the FHIR API and event-data plumbing.

Coverage-Loss & Bad-Debt Analytics

Get the authorization approved — then make sure the money comes in.

Six-month Medicaid renewals and the expiration of enhanced ACA subsidies mean more patients losing coverage.
