This data platform modernization case study covers work our founder led before
starting ByteStreams, at a medical device manufacturer. It's the same approach we
now bring to client engagements.

## The Challenge

The company's data lived in separate worlds. Manufacturing data came from its
plants, and surgery data came from procedures across the country. Each team had
its own reports, and they didn't agree.

Without those sources joined, nobody could say with confidence which items a given
surgery would actually use. Kits sent to surgery were sized for the worst case,
which meant more inventory in the field, heavier logistics, and more cleaning and
reprocessing before and after each procedure.

## Our Approach

Our founder designed the platform's technology stack, then led the team through
development, implementation, training and deployments.

The platform brought manufacturing and surgery data together into a single
2.8 PB data lake, modeled so the business could see an accurate usage mix for each
type of surgery: which items were used, in what sizes, and how often. That one
trusted source then served the whole company:

- **Supply chain:** right-sizing surgical kits and inventory across logistics
  locations
- **Sales:** incentives and territory performance for the field sales team
- **Leadership:** tracking company goals on numbers everyone agreed on
- **Compliance and training:** consistent, auditable data behind both

## Results

The biggest win came from an unglamorous number: an accurate usage mix per
surgery. Once the business trusted it, kits got smaller, inventory came down,
logistics and reprocessing got lighter, and surgeons got a better experience.
Sales, supply chain and leadership stopped arguing over whose numbers were right.
